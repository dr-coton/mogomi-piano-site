Privacy policy

You do not need to send a child’s name or photo when contacting us.

How do we begin?

Choose Hot Cross Buns from the song list and start Practice. Each note waits until you find its key. When you are ready, try playing along with falling notes in Rhythm mode.

How do I open grown-up settings?

Tap the grown-up lock on Home. Add the two numbers written in words and enter the sum. Settings include note names, starting speed, volume and clearing stars.

The notes are too fast for my child.

Choose 50% speed on the song list and begin with Practice. Level 1 tunes use five notes from C to G. Place the tablet sideways and explore slowly together.

Why is there no sound?

Check the device media volume and the volume in grown-up settings. Also check whether audio is going to headphones or a Bluetooth device. The app never records your child’s voice.

Where are stars and settings stored?

They stay in app-private device storage. Clear stars removes stars and practice marks, while preferences remain. For OS backups and app removal, see sections 2 and 3 of the privacy policy.

Do we need the internet or an account?

No. Songs and sounds are included. There are no ads, accounts, tracking tools or network connections made by the app.

Does it teach acoustic-piano fingering?

It is playful screen-keyboard practice for note names and starting beats. It is not a replacement for acoustic-piano fingering lessons.
