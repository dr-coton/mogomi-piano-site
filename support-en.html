Privacy policy

You do not need to send a child’s name or photo when contacting us.

How do we begin?

Choose Hot Cross Buns from the song list and start Practice. Each note waits until you find its key. When you are ready, try playing along with falling notes in Rhythm mode.

How do I open settings?

Tap Settings on the Home screen. Children can open and change settings directly. Options include note names, starting speed, volume, clearing stars, and Restore purchase (after a grown-up check).

The notes are too fast for my child.

Choose 50% speed on the song list and begin with Practice. Level 1 tunes use five notes from C to G. Place the tablet sideways and explore slowly together.

Why is there no sound?

Check the device media volume and the volume in settings. Also check whether audio is going to headphones or a Bluetooth device. The app never records your child’s voice.

Where are stars and settings stored?

Stars, practice marks, Beyer theme-completion marks for pieces 1 and 2, and the piece number opened through the grown-up shortcut stay in app-private device storage. Clear stars removes stars, practice and theme marks; grown-up shortcuts and settings remain. Back to one by one in the grown-up menu removes the shortcut while keeping progress records. See privacy policy sections 2 and 3 for backups and app removal.

How do I open the Beyer pieces, and how do I buy them?

Start from Beyer Land on Home or its entry card in the song list. Earn at least one star or finish Practice to open the next piece. Completing the introductory themes of pieces 1 and 2 also opens the next piece. Pieces 1–10 are free; a single purchase gives access to 11–106 (no subscription). Pieces still open in order. At the top right of the map, Grown-up menu → multiplication check lets you open up to any piece or open them all. Tapping a locked piece shows a simple message for children; the price and buy button appear only after a grown-up check (a two-digit × one-digit multiplication question). Purchases and restores also start only after that check. There are no purchase prompts during play. Payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play, and the developer receives no payment details. If you reinstall or switch devices with the same store account, tap Restore purchase at the bottom of Settings (Family Sharing is supported on iPhone and iPad).

Do we need the internet or an account?

No. Songs and sounds are included, so no internet is needed to play. There are no ads, accounts, or tracking, and the app sends nothing to the developer. Buying and restoring require the device’s store connection. When the app opens, it checks the store for purchase status and price, but sends nothing from that check to the developer.

Does it teach acoustic-piano fingering?

It is playful screen-keyboard practice for note names and starting beats. It is not a replacement for acoustic-piano fingering lessons.
