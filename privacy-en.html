Mogomi Piano Privacy Policy

English

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Mogomi Piano (the “app”) is a music-play app by Code Black Inc. for children aged 4 to 8, on iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets. The app does not collect personal information or send it to developer servers or third parties. This policy distinguishes the app’s behavior from device backups or email support that a grown-up separately chooses to use.

1. Information we do not collect

The app does not collect names, birthdays, photos, voices, location, contacts, device identifiers or usage history. There are no accounts, logins, advertising, analytics or tracking tools. The app itself makes no internet connections and does not access the camera, microphone, photos or contacts. Touch input is used only to play sounds and calculate the current game’s progress. The app does not record, store or transmit a history of playing or touches.

2. Information kept on the device

The app stores each song’s best stars, practice-completion marks, and preferences for note labels, speed, volume, selected instrument, selected theme, locking theme changes, backing music on/off, locking instrument changes, vibration on/off, and the break-reminder interval (off/10/15/20 minutes) in app-private storage. Versions that offer voice guidance also save its on/off preference on the device. The first release has no guidance recordings and does not use or save this preference. On iPhone and iPad, it also saves the choice between larger keys and all keys. On Android, that keyboard choice is state for the current playing screen, not a saved app preference. The app also saves on the device whether the first-song guide was completed and the grown-up welcome was viewed. It does not create profiles containing names or birthdays. These items are kept only in app-private local storage; the app does not send them outside the device to the developer or third parties. Elapsed time calculated for break reminders stays in memory while the app runs; it is not saved as usage history on the device or transmitted elsewhere.

On iPhone and iPad, device backups to iCloud or a computer may include stars and settings (including the selected theme and theme-change lock), depending on Apple’s backup settings and policies. The app does not provide its own iCloud synchronization. On Android, the app is configured to exclude its stored progress and settings from automatic backup and device-to-device transfer. Those backup and transfer services follow their own policies.

3. Keeping and deleting

Stars and settings (including the selected theme and theme-change lock) remain until a grown-up clears them or removes the app. To reset stars and practice-completion marks, open Home → Grown-ups → solve the addition question written in words → Clear stars. This does not reset other preferences or the marks showing that guidance has been viewed. On Android, clearing the app’s storage in system settings removes both progress and preferences. On iPhone and iPad, fully deleting the app removes its data from that device; offloading an app can retain data. Existing device backups are not deleted by these actions, and restoring a backup may bring saved data back.

4. Sharing, saving and web links

The current app has no recording, export, sharing, printing, photo-saving or external web-link features. A grown-up can read the privacy policy offline in settings. Opening a store support or privacy webpage outside the app involves that website’s normal communications; it is not data sent by the app.

5. Sound

Piano, marimba (xylophone), music-box, chord-pad, bass, bell, applause and count-in sounds are all originally created and synthesized files included in the app. Playback does not connect to the internet. The app does not record or analyze a child’s voice or a real piano.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is made for children and does not collect or share personal information from children or their parents. Grown-up settings and this policy sit behind an addition question written in words. This check controls access to settings; it is not a process for obtaining consent to collect personal information.

7. Changes

If the app’s features or information practices change, we will update this policy, its effective date and the explanation shown in the app.

8. Contact

Operator: Code Black Inc. A grown-up may use the developer contact on the Mogomi Piano App Store or Google Play page, or email dr.coton@gmail.com. If you send an email, the address and message you provide are handled through email services so we can respond. You do not need to include a child’s name, photo, voice or other personal information. Support email is not information that the app automatically collects or sends.
